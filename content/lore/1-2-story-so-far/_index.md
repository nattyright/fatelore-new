### Season 0/1 (2104)
{{< details title="**Spoilers**" open=false >}}
Takes place in a nomadic city called `Persepolis` suffering a slow decline due to the conflict between the city's aristocracy and mafia. The mafia brags about having a Holy Grail, and a Holy Grail War begins. The winners of the war end up being the Rider team of **Pascal Poirot** and **Neil Armstrong**. A Church representative interferes in the finale, grabbing the grail, which is revealed to be a falsehood and a bomb. The bomb goes off, powerful enough to cause lasting damage to Persepolis. The city is evacuated and put up for auction.
{{< /details >}}



### Season 2 (2104)
{{< details title="**Spoilers**" open=false >}}
Takes place in `Olympia` and `Delphi`, which bid on the ownership of `Persepolis`. As a compromise, the two elect to host a Grail War to decide who gets it instead. The winners of the war are **Gustave** and **Simo Hayha**. Delphi wins Persepolis and prepares to upgrade to a Stella Maris city.
{{< /details >}}



### Season 3 (2104-2105)
{{< details title="**Spoilers**" open=false >}}
A group of investigators chase down a *nature reserve*-type nomadic city that went off the grid after attempting to make contact with the land beneath the ocean. Instead, the city made contact with the Grail Mud, which corrupted the inhabitants and one of the city’s Servants. Two separate investigation teams are sent to `Nirvana`, but they fail and never come back. Season 3 focuses on the *third team* and the results of their expedition. Since Nirvana’s citizens are influenced by the mud, they're aggressive towards outsiders and attempt to thwart the investigators’ goals to discover the truth. 

The investigators gradually learn about the Grail Mud, and receive the assistance of a Servant who appears to be **William Shakespeare**. The investigators eventually come into conflict with a Servant called **Vortigern**, and it turns out that William Shakespeare is actually **William Henry Ireland**, and Vortigern is a corrupted **El Cid**. Regardless, the investigators manage to defeat William and discover the truth of what happened at Nirvana. They escape and warn the outside world.
{{< /details >}}



### Season 3.5 (2069)
{{< details title="**Spoilers**" open=false >}}
Takes place in `Persepolis`, several decades before the events of Season 0/1. A select group of Servants is summoned to a sanctioned site within the city, designated by Republic as the 25th beta test of the S.E.R.V.A.N.T. frames they engineered. The summoned Servants are instructed to fight each other to the death, all to allow Republic to rigorously evaluate the capabilities and stability of the frames.
{{< /details >}}



### Season 4 (2105)
{{< details title="**Spoilers**" open=false >}}
Takes place on `Alexandria`, the host of ServantPedia™️ in the new world. [[13.1 Republic|Republic]] invades them to take control of their information archives for vague and undisclosed reasons. A tournament is held between Alexandria and Republic to decide the outcome of their claim, with the [[13.2 Neo Holy Empire|Neo Holy Empire]] taking special interest in it as they attempt to support Alexandria. The Neo Holy Empire sends a *Heavenly General*, and Republic sends a resummoned *William Henry Ireland* and a *Republic envoy* to broker a deal with Alexandria. Meanwhile, a Servant affiliated with Alexandria named **F. Scott Fitzgerald** disguised as Jay Gatsby attempts to stop the Grail Mud on his own. The winner of the war is Alexandria, and Republic is forced to pull back.
{{< /details >}}



### Season 4.5 (1999-2000)
{{< details title="**Spoilers**" open=false >}}
Takes place in `Berenice`, an island on the Nile in Upper Egypt. The Church detects unusual leyline activities, leading them to believe the advent of an irregular Holy Grail War. The Church dispatches priest **Dionysius Andreas** and Executor **Gabrielle** as overseers. They are joined by Sister **Rosula** and **Astro Boy**, a frail-to-the-touch ‘Ruler’ Class Servant appointed to referee the War.

As the War goes on, Astro Boy’s health starts to improve suspiciously with each unexplained death. He reveals himself to be **Laplace’s Demon**, an entity once under the care of the Atlas Institute. The Demon had foreseen a *world-ending catastrophe in 2004* and escaped after causing a country-wide blackout. With a plan to use the Grail’s power to rewrite the world and thus avert the disaster, the Demon consumes both Church overseers and hijacks the Saint Graph of Rider **Sri Kalki**, then aligns with Kalki’s Master, **Illyn B. Mariston**.

Together, Master and new Servant use Sister Rosula’s body as a vessel to call forth the Grail, but Illyn has a change of heart. He argues that rather than relying on the Demon to play god, humanity should confront its impending doom with their own resilience and strength. The Demon acknowledges Illyn’s perspective and agrees to leave humanity to face its fate. It uses the Grail’s power to restore the War to its original trajectory, then vanishes from Berenice, leaving behind a nearly depleted Holy Grail with just enough power for one final wish.

The Holy Grail War concludes with **James Rellum** and his Servant, Assassin **Triboulet**, as the winners.
{{< /details >}}



### Event 1 - HHS Fes (2106)
{{< details title="**Spoilers**" open=false >}}
Takes place in `Alexandria`, which hosts an annual festival celebrating all forms of knowledge, though more popularly those of the sciences and arts. During the festival, several characters (mages and Servants) have bizarre and unique encounters with the various stalls, ranging from a shop that sells all sorts of strange paraphernalia, to a race between two Servants to the Alexandrian docks.

Eventually, the festival concludes, and people wait until the time of the next HHS Festival…
{{< /details >}}



### Season 5 (2077)
{{< details title="**Spoilers**" open=false >}}
Takes place in `Mont Saint-Michel de Epoque`, a city that reported the first [[11.6 The Holy Grail|'nomadic Grail']] sighting in 2077.  News of the Grail attracts attention from potential Servant/Master candidates and the Neo Holy Empire, resulting in a proper Holy Grail War being hosted for the first time in years. Unbeknownst to all, however, Epoque's [[12.2 Genesis Spirits|Genesis Spirit]] has been controlled by an AI known as *Incognita*, who deems the Grail's presence as a threat to Epoque. 

Incognita sends a group of Servants (**Galileo Galilei (Alter)/ Little Red Riding Hood/ Leshy**) to confront the HGW participants, but its mismanagement of its Servants results in **Leshy** manipulating **Red Hood** and **Galileo** into summoning a priestess for an outer god, **Nodens.** Nodens attempts to claim the Grail in order to manifest itself into the human world, but is thwarted by the combined efforts of Incognita, the HGW participants, and remaining Servant NPCs.

The war ends with no winners, as the world is saved by the Grail's wish and Epoque's citizens forget all about the Genesis Spirit and Nodens. However, traces of what happened remain, as **Edward Hyodou** and **Ismene Drakon** are granted a wish from the Grail by Nodens' priestess, before she disappears and leaves this world.
{{< /details >}}



### Event 2 - Masquerade (2105)
{{< details title="**Spoilers**" open=false >}}
Takes place in `redacted`, a grand mansion in the heart of `Mont Saint-Michel de Epoque`. For one reason or another, Masters and Servants across space and time receive an invitation, before finding themselves gathered at the mansion for a grand masquerade. 

The masquerade is hosted by the illustrious **Gill Bates**, sef-appointed CEO of [[10 Lore (Public)/19 Embeds/Known Locations/Delphi|Delphi]], who has just won the City Grail War of 2104. As the night goes on, a pair of assassins reveal themselves: an Assassin Class Servant, **Masque of the Red Death**, and a Caster Class Servant, **King in Yellow**. They both unleash their Noble Phantasms–a deadly plague spread by the Masque and the mansion that’s turned hostile by the King–both aimed at killing Gill Bates.

In an attempt to thwart the assassination, the guests work together and manage to flee. Just as the assassins vanish, everyone awakens in their respective time periods, only to discover that the entire night was merely a dream. Somewhere, a mysterious entity reveals themselves, having observed the night’s events from afar.
{{< /details >}}
