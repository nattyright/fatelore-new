# A City's Core

While most humans know of nomadic cities as being powered by sea-faring runes and [[NAT|NATs]], there is actually a third element at play to sustain a nomadic city's structure and grandeur. Every rune attached to a nomadic city is actually an engraving that represents a certain **animal species**, and a physical representation of said species is usually found in the deepest parts of a nomadic city, inside its own core. These entities are known as **Genesis Spirits.**

So long as a Genesis Spirit exists and is attached to a nomadic city, the structural integrity of the city greatly increases, along with the city's overall authority. The city (and its Genesis Spirit) becomes capable of withstanding **S.E.R.V.A.N.T.-level attacks** and will only collapse once most of its resources and NATs have been depleted *OR* if the Genesis Spirit is killed. 

{{< hint "info" >}}
Genesis Spirits are **not common knowledge** in the *Starry Night* setting, and are not documented anywhere in the world at the moment. The reason for this page is to give people lore context on their existence, but 99% of RP characters would be unaware of the presence of a Genesis Spirit in their nomadic city.
{{< /hint >}}

# Genesis Spirit Behaviour

Genesis Spirits are very protective of their territory and rarely, if ever, seen by a city's human inhabitants. People often do not notice their presence, and even so-called owners of nomadic cities believe that NATs are fueling a nomadic city's various components instead of realizing that they are feeding the Genesis Spirit as well. Because of this, Genesis Spirits are usually **quiet observers,** content with monitoring the city's life and condition from a distance.

Most Genesis Spirits subtly influence the city's performance in the direction they prefer, if there is none, but are otherwise more than happy to let others take the reigns. They are the ultimate fall back plan in case a city is devoid of a leader, and also the ultimate defense against anything that would attempt to compromise a city from within.

Because a Genesis Spirit is so attached to a nomadic city, its life is inevitably linked to it. Destruction of a nomadic city results in the **death** of a Genesis Spirit as well.

{{< hint "info" >}}
A Genesis Spirit is elusive. Each Genesis Spirit exhibits different behaviors, have different preferences on where and when it decides to show up, if at all. Most of the time, it's not meant to be an element that can be casually interacted with despite being an important piece of lore. 
{{< /hint >}}

# Fusion between nomadic cities
It is common for nomadic cities to merge with another nomadic city, be it via a mutual agreement, aggressive conquering, or simply salvaging remains from a wreck. Genesis Spirits are also involved in this process, with the spirits from the respective cities **devouring one another** in the process of a merge.

During this process, the Genesis Spirit **from the bigger city** is often the one that ends up as the **dominant species.** It will devour the spirit of the lesser city and inherit some of its traits, resulting in a **hybrid Genesis Spirit** with dominant traits belonging to the *'winning'* spirit while the *'loser'* spirit has minor aspects of themselves pass on to this new hybrid.

If two cities 'fused' without producing a hybrid Genesis Spirit, then you are most likely thinking of `Docking`. When salvaging the destroyed remains of a nomadic city, this fusion **does not happen.** If a city is destroyed, it means the Genesis Spirit associated with that city is already dead, meaning there is nothing to merge with.

> `Docking`
>  
> When two cities join together without having their Genesis Spirits devour one another, the process is called 'Docking'. It's simply a case of using good engineering to temporarily bridge the two cities so people can move between them. It's very useful for events like festivals and hosting City Grail Wars (Season 2).


{{< hint "warning" >}}
### Exceptions
While the smaller city often loses when it comes to merges, a small city **with more NATs than the bigger city** will actually result in the smaller Genesis Spirit being victorious due to its power reserves, resulting in the bigger Genesis Spirit being devoured instead. This has no impact on the bigger city, however, besides replacing their dominant Genesis Spirit with the one from the smaller city.
{{< /hint >}}

# FAQ

{{< hint "info" >}}
### What cities have Genesis Spirits?
All Stella Maris, Cynosura, and Dhruva cities have Genesis Spirits.
Some Polaris cities have Genesis Spirits. But many others do not (just regular ships). If a Polaris has a Genesis Spirit, then the size of the Polaris should be reasonably large (mega ships)
All nomadic cities with Genesis Spirits were built using a *blueprint*.
{{< /hint >}}

{{< hint "info" >}}
### How many Genesis Spirits are out there?
/Starry Night started out with roughly 2000 Genesis Spirits. Now that number has dwindled to less than 1000.
